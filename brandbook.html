Brand book — case study

EVOLVE

Evolve Business Group is a diversified holding company spanning import and export, transport, construction, and industry. They came to us with an identity that had been generated rather than designed — playful type, inconsistent kerning, and a mark that didn’t carry the authority of the business behind it.

We kept the symbol, because the client was attached to it and it was worth keeping. Everything around it was rebuilt: archetype, positioning, a full logo system across four layouts, a two-colour palette, a typeface pairing, and the application rules that keep it consistent long after we hand it over.
