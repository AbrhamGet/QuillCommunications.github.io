The process

What a campaign looks like

  1. 1

    Match

    Creator archetype first, follower count second, judged on tone, audience, and whether the endorsement would be believable.

  2. 2

    Negotiate

    Rates, deliverables, usage rights, and timeline are agreed in writing before anyone shoots.

  3. 3

    Brief

    A brief that leaves room for the creator's own voice. Over-scripted influencer content reads as an advertisement and performs like one.

  4. 4

    Manage

    We chase, review, and request revisions, so you never have that conversation.

  5. 5

    Report

    Reach, engagement, and what the campaign actually drove, rather than a screenshot of the post.
