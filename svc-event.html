Service

Event Documentation

Launches, openings, and events filmed live, then edited fast enough to still be relevant.

What this is

Filmed on the night. Posted while it still matters.

An event has a short window. People talk about it for roughly two days, and then they stop. We shoot on the day and turn the edit around fast enough to land inside that window, rather than delivering a polished recap a fortnight after anyone cared.

Selected coverage

Strictly Soul

A full event campaign: eleven pieces of content before the night to move tickets, six after to stretch the moment. The work reached 1.19M views at a 3.02% engagement rate, though the number that mattered to the client was a full room.

Formats

How it scales

  1. 1

    Single camera

    One operator, roaming. Suited to openings, small launches, and intimate settings.

  2. 2

    Multi-camera

    Several angles plus a dedicated stills shooter. Built for concerts, conferences, and anything with a stage.

  3. 3

    Same-day turnaround

    A short cut delivered that night or the following morning, while the event is still live in the feed.

  4. 4

    Full recap package

    The long edit, the vertical cuts, and a stills set, delivered across the following week.
