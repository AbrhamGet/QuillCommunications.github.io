Video Production

Shooting and editing, nothing else. You bring the plan, we film it properly and hand back the cut.

What this is

Shooting and editing. That is the scope.

You develop the content and you publish it. We supply the camera, the crew, and the edit. This suits brands that already know what they want to say and need it filmed properly. Where footage already exists, we will cut it.

Who attends

The crew

Three roles cover a standard shoot. Larger productions scale up from there.

Production Director

Runs the day: shot list, pacing, and the call on whether we have the take.

Videographer

Shoots and cuts. Framing, camera movement, and the final edit.

Gaffer

Lighting. The reason footage looks like it cost more than it did.

Post-production

The edit

Each job is cut in whichever suite it calls for. Heavy colour work goes through Resolve, layered edits through Premiere, and fast social turnarounds through CapCut. Delivery comes in whatever format and aspect ratio you need, whether vertical for TikTok and Reels, square for feed, or wide for YouTube and event screens.
