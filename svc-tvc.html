Service

TVC — Television Commercials

Broadcast-grade commercial production, from concept and script through the shoot to the final master.

What this is

The most expensive-looking work a brand produces.

A TVC is the piece a brand gets judged on. It runs on television, ahead of a YouTube video, or on a screen at an event, in each case competing with something the viewer already chose to watch. We handle it from the first concept conversation through to the delivered master.

The process

How a TVC gets made

  1. 1

    Brief and concept

    What the advertisement has to achieve, who it addresses, and the single idea it rests on. We return with concepts rather than a schedule.

  2. 2

    Script and storyboard

    The spot is written to length, then boarded shot by shot so no problem is discovered on the shoot day.

  3. 3

    Pre-production

    Casting, location, permits, kit list, and call sheet. The unglamorous stage that decides whether the shoot runs on time.

  4. 4

    The shoot

    Cinema camera, controlled lighting, and monitored sound, directed against the storyboards with room to take a better idea when one appears.

  5. 5

    Post-production

    Edit, colour grade, sound design, and mix, with graphics and titles where the spot calls for them.

  6. 6

    Delivery

    A broadcast-specification master, plus cutdowns in vertical, square, and shortened versions for wherever else it runs.
