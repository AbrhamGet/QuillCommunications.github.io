Service

TVC — Television Commercials

Broadcast-grade commercial production: concept, script, shoot, and the final master.

What this is

The most expensive-looking thing a brand makes.

A TVC is the piece a brand gets judged on. It runs on television, in front of a YouTube video, or on a screen at an event — places where people are already watching something else and yours has to hold its own. We handle it end to end, from the first concept conversation to the delivered master.

The process

How a TVC gets made

  1. 1

    Brief and concept

    What the ad has to achieve, who it's talking to, and the single idea it hangs on. We come back with concepts, not a schedule.

  2. 2

    Script and storyboard

    The spot written to length, then boarded shot by shot so nobody discovers a problem on the day.

  3. 3

    Pre-production

    Casting, location, permits, kit list, and a call sheet. The unglamorous part that decides whether the shoot runs on time.

  4. 4

    The shoot

    Cinema camera, controlled lighting, monitored sound. Directed against the boards, with room to take the better idea when it turns up.

  5. 5

    Post-production

    Edit, colour grade, sound design, and mix. Graphics and titles where the spot needs them.

  6. 6

    Delivery

    Broadcast-spec master plus the cutdowns — vertical, square, and short versions for wherever else it runs.
