Community Management

Comments, messages, and channel communities handled daily, in your voice rather than a template.

What this is

The least glamorous part of social media, and the part that decides whether people trust you.

Comments and messages accumulate while you run the business. Someone asks a price at 9pm and receives an answer three days later, or never. We reply daily in your brand's voice and flag the cases that genuinely need you.

Day to day

What we handle

  1. 1

    Comments

    Answered, moderated, and steered, including the difficult ones, which are usually where trust is won or lost.

  2. 2

    Direct messages

    Product questions, price requests, and complaints, handled to a script agreed with you in advance.

  3. 3

    Escalation

    Anything requiring a real decision reaches you with context attached, rather than sitting in an inbox.

  4. 4

    Channel communities

    Telegram groups and channels are kept active rather than left to go quiet between posts.
