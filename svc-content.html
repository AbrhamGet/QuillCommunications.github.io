Content Creation

Scripts, concepts, and the thinking behind what gets posted. Built to interrupt a scroll rather than fill a calendar.

What this is

You keep the account. We supply the ideas.

Where a company already runs its own social media and shoots its own video, we work behind that team as the creative engine. Concepts, scripts, and direction through post-production, with your people keeping the camera and the calendar. If no strategy exists yet, we build one first and hand it to whoever executes.

How we work

The Content Playbook

Every script is measured against an internal standard before it reaches production. Six of those principles:

01The 3-second rule

If the opening does not produce a “wait, what?” reaction, the video has already failed. Nothing enters production without clearing this.

02The Mom Test

Would the opening hold someone with no prior interest in the product? A hook that only works on people already sold is not a hook.

03Open loops

The opening plants a question the viewer can only answer by staying. Curiosity consistently outperforms information.

04Breadcrumb pacing

The payoff is never delivered at once. A small reveal every few seconds stops attention from leaking away.

05Natural Habesha tone

Scripts are written in the Amharic-English blend people in Addis actually speak, so they read as a recommendation from a peer rather than a lecture from a brand.

06Mute-friendly by default

Dynamic captions with key words highlighted, since most of the feed is watched with the sound off.

The feedback loop

What we measure

Creativity without measurement is ego. Short-form work is held to retention benchmarks covering the first three seconds, total watch duration, and engagement rate. Every major campaign launches with more than one hook, so the winner is chosen by performance rather than by taste.

  1. 1

    Write against the standard

    The hook, the body, and the conversion layer each carry their own criteria. A script clears all of them before production begins.

  2. 2

    Test multiple hooks

    Identical body, identical call to action, different openings. The market decides which was right.

  3. 3

    Read the retention curve

    The point where viewers drop identifies which section failed: the opening, the middle, or the ask.

  4. 4

    Iterate on the winner

    The strongest opening becomes the template and gets pushed further the following round.
