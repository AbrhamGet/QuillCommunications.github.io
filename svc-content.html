How we work

The Content Playbook

Every script we write is measured against an internal standard before it goes into production. These are the parts of it we're happy to show:

01The 3-second rule

If the opening doesn't force a “wait, what?” reaction, the video has already failed. Every script is vetted before it reaches production.

02The Mom Test

Would this hold someone with zero prior interest in the product? If a hook only works on people already sold, it isn't a hook.

03Open loops

The opening should plant a question the viewer can only answer by staying. Curiosity outperforms information.

04Breadcrumb pacing

Never deliver the whole payoff at once. A small reveal every few seconds keeps attention from leaking.

05Natural Habesha tone

No translated-sounding Amharic, no lecture voice. Scripts read like a recommendation from a peer, in the Amharic-English blend people in Addis actually speak.

06Mute-friendly by default

Dynamic captions with key words highlighted, because most of the feed is watched with the sound off.

The feedback loop

What we measure

Creativity without measurement is ego. Short-form work is held to real retention numbers — 3-second retention, watch duration, and engagement — and every major campaign launches with two or three hook variations so the winner is chosen by data rather than taste.

  1. 1

    Write against the standard

    Hook, body, and conversion layers each have their own criteria. Nothing enters production without passing all three.

  2. 2

    Test multiple hooks

    Same body, same call to action, different openings. The market decides which one was right.

  3. 3

    Read the retention curve

    Where people drop tells you which part failed — the hook, the middle, or the ask.

  4. 4

    Iterate on the winner

    The strongest hook becomes the template, then gets pushed further next round.
