The process

How a strategy gets built

Before anything gets posted, the account gets a strategy document. This is the shape of it — the same ten questions every time, answered specifically for your business:

01
Objective

What the account is actually for — sales, awareness, or audience — and what success looks like in numbers rather than adjectives.

02
The big idea

One dominant emotional lane. Trying to be everything at once is the fastest way to become forgettable.

03
Audience segmentation

Not “women 18–35.” Who buys for themselves, who buys as a gift, what each one hesitates over, and what answers that hesitation.

04
Differentiation

Why this brand rather than the identical one next to it. If the answer is “quality and good service,” there is no answer yet.

05
Content pillars

Every post sits under a pillar — storytelling, social proof, education, or conversion. If it doesn't, it's noise.

06
Platform approach

Same message, different psychology. What's discovery content, what's conversion content, and how often each platform gets used.

07
Creators and UGC

Creator archetypes rather than follower counts, and a tone that fits the brand instead of the creator's usual feed.

08
Offer strategy

Bundles, limited editions, and urgency built on something real — discounting without a story cheapens a brand fast.

09
Conversion path

Clear calls to action per content type, and an honest look at where the buying journey has friction.

10
Phasing

A month is not one moment. Anticipation, peak, and follow-through get different content.
